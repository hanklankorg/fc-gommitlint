# Simple Contributor Code of Conduct

## Version 1.0

We expect participants to:

- **Be respectful and considerate**: Treat others with courtesy and respect.
- **Assume good faith**: Start from the belief that others intend to help.
- **Provide constructive feedback**: Focus feedback on improving the project.
- **Focus on the work**: Debate ideas, not people. Disagreement is normal, and
  people may decline further discussion.
- **Acknowledge mistakes**: Apologize when necessary and learn from errors.
- **Keep discussions on topic**: Keep project spaces focused on the project,
  its users, and its contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, slurs, threats, or targeted degradation.
- Personal attacks or demeaning behavior.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.

We interpret this Code of Conduct charitably. Honest mistakes,
miscommunication, and isolated frustration should be addressed through
conversation.

Concerns may be reported privately to <janderssonse@proton.me>. Reports are
handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions
that violate this Code of Conduct and may restrict participation when needed.
